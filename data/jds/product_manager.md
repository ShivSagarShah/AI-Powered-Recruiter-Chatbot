# Product Manager

## Company
Harbor Labs

## Location
Seattle, WA

## Summary
Harbor Labs is hiring a Product Manager to drive roadmap execution for our internal workflow platform. The successful candidate will turn user feedback, customer needs, and initiative goals into clear product direction and measurable outcomes.

## Responsibilities
- Define product vision, requirements, and roadmap for platform features.
- Translate customer insights into prioritized opportunities and measurable goals.
- Coordinate cross-functional delivery with engineering, design, sales, and support.
- Measure feature impact using product analytics and customer feedback.
- Create clear communication and launch plans for stakeholders across the business.

## Requirements
- 4+ years of product management or product operations experience.
- Experience working with software products and cross-functional technical teams.
- Ability to analyze product metrics and make decisions with incomplete information.
- Strong stakeholder management and written communication abilities.
- Experience with agile delivery practices and product analytics tools.
