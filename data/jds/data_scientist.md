# Data Scientist

## Company
Northstar Analytics

## Location
New York, NY

## Summary
Northstar Analytics is looking for a Data Scientist to turn complex datasets into actionable product intelligence. This role blends experimentation, statistical analysis, and machine learning to support product and customer strategy.

## Responsibilities
- Build predictive models and analytical frameworks for customer behavior and product performance.
- Design and analyze A/B tests to quantify impact and business value.
- Partner with engineers and product managers to define metrics and monitor key trends.
- Communicate findings and recommendations to stakeholders in clear, practical language.
- Maintain data quality checks and documentation for reproducible analysis.

## Requirements
- 3+ years of experience in data analysis or applied machine learning.
- Proficiency in Python and SQL for exploratory analysis and model development.
- Experience with scikit-learn, pandas, and statistical inference.
- Strong communication and storytelling skills for technical and non-technical audiences.
- Ability to work in cross-functional teams and balance speed with rigor.
