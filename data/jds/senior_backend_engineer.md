# Senior Backend Engineer

## Company
Acme Cloud

## Location
Remote · United States

## Summary
We are seeking a Senior Backend Engineer to design, build, and scale robust APIs and distributed services for our cloud platform. The ideal candidate is comfortable working across microservices, event-driven architectures, and observability tooling.

## Responsibilities
- Design and maintain backend services powering customer-facing APIs and internal tooling.
- Own service reliability, performance tuning, and incident response for production systems.
- Collaborate with product, frontend, and data teams to define technical requirements.
- Write clean, testable code with strong documentation and monitoring.
- Guide architectural decisions and mentor junior engineers.

## Requirements
- 5+ years of experience building production backend services in Python, Go, or Java.
- Strong knowledge of REST APIs, SQL databases, and distributed systems.
- Experience with cloud platforms such as AWS, GCP, or Azure.
- Familiarity with container orchestration, CI/CD, and observability tools.
- Excellent debugging and communication skills.
